## Findings and concise Codex Sol handoff

1. **P1 — actual provider restriction/dependency loss.** The idle Lumber Yard response omitted `PreferredWorkers IdleOnly`; its admitted goal therefore had `UseIdleWorkersOnly=false`. The compound response omitted the requested `dependsOn:[0]` and ran worker assignment early. First failing layer is provider interpretation, before planner policy. The provider instruction already says how idle builders should be represented ([OpenRouterCommanderProvider.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/AI/Commander/Phase4A/OpenRouterCommanderProvider.cs), line 32); the parser permits node constraints and dependencies ([CommanderSemanticJson.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/AI/Commander/Phase4E/CommanderSemanticJson.cs), lines 128 and 193), and supplied IdleOnly is enforced by `CaptureConstraints`/`CanReassignWorker` ([CommanderPlanner.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/AI/Commander/CommanderPlanner.cs), lines 261–312). Improve and verify the ordinary provider contract's explicit constraint and dependency coverage. Preserve typed structural authorization, deterministic worker selection and plan confirmation; do not paper over this with English phrase checks, strategy blacklists, automatic approval or relaxed validation.
2. **P1 — idle-Mill provider/schema failure.** HTTP 200 produced invalid semantics and no admission; the UI truthfully rejected it. Reproduce with safe schema diagnostics before choosing a repair. Exact offending field is **not proven** by this audit; neither the provider nor parser should be changed based on a guessed field. The later 15-second cancellation is an independent evidence gap. Do not increase timeouts/retries or weaken semantic assertions to manufacture acceptance.
3. **P1 — progress mixes two counting bases.** `SubmitBuildStructure`/graph creation sets `TargetTotal` to completed baseline plus requested count ([CommanderGoalManager.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/AI/Commander/CommanderGoalManager.cs), lines 164 and 260). The bound semantic-placement completion branch reports **one exact placed result** ([CommanderPlanner.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/AI/Commander/CommanderPlanner.cs), lines 404–425). `GoalProgress` divides that local observation by the global `TargetTotal` ([CommanderTaskBoardProjection.cs](../../Assets/Scripts/AI/Commander/Phase5B/CommanderTaskBoardProjection.cs), lines 238–239), yielding Completed 1/2. Use a consistent per-request or global counting basis, with pre-existing and repeated-structure coverage. Retain completion based on the real finished building, not assignment.

After fixes, repeat the exact idle construction and compound wording with captured typed fields, including an unavailable-idle-worker case, and replay repeated same-type buildings. Preserve these first failures and timeout attempts; a later passing retry does not erase them.
