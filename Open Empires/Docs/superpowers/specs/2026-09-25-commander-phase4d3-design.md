An approved plan's existing deterministic goals may wait, gather, construct ordinary prerequisites, retry, and resume without another approval while remaining inside its objective, template, targets, and budget policy. A health observation is not permission to change any of those. Phase 4D.3 does not fix the separately reported tactical Spearman wood-gathering failure or infer its cause from a generic worker-unavailable string.

Any new strategic request while a plan is active is treated as material, even when the proposed template is compatible. The proposal is an observation and player-facing choice, not a plan, intent submission, reservation, or command. A normal AI recommendation cannot supersede an incompatible active plan. The explicit **Confirm as command** action is the player's replacement authority, subject to the existing approval, decision, commitment, and planner checks. An AI-confirmed request may supersede AI-owned conflicting plans but may not supersede a direct-player plan. Compatible plans may coexist; the UI must not promise replacement in that case.

## Alternatives and choice

1. Let the provider or a health classifier call `StrategicPlanner.SubmitIntent` when a wait persists: rejected. It crosses the authority boundary and confuses temporary recovery with material re-planning.
2. Store the new intent as a pending detached proposal and reuse the existing host confirmation path: selected. The proposal copies evidence, the bridge retains the pending identity, and only an explicit player action may submit through `StrategicApprovalLayer` → `StrategicPipeline.EvaluateApprovedIntentNow` → `IStrategicApprovedDecisionPolicy.DecideApproved` → `StrategicPlanner.SubmitIntent`.
3. Add arbitrary target/budget parameters or a second replacement API: rejected for this phase. Present templates and `StrategicApprovalLayer` do not validate those changes. Unsupported increases must fail closed, not acquire implicit authority.

## Host behavior

The strategic route in `CommanderChatUI` continues to use the existing bridge and buttons. With an active plan, successful translation displays a bounded proposal naming the source plan and proposed objective, explicitly stating the old plan remains active. The ordinary Approve button is not a replacement action; it must not consume an adaptation proposal. Confirm as command revalidates the proposal **before** consuming the pending bridge identity, then uses the existing approval/pipeline path. No automatic recovery flow creates a proposal merely because health reports `WaitingForResources`, `WaitingForPopulation`, or a temporarily blocked child. Existing deterministic milestones carry those cases.

Keep multi-plan and same-objective semantics honest. A proposal can say a new plan may coexist or supersede according to existing commitment policy; it cannot guarantee replacement before policy evaluation. If the incoming objective or parameter is unsupported by current validation, reject without modifying the active plan. Never silently lower an approved target, raise a budget, add an attack/move behavior, or change priority.
