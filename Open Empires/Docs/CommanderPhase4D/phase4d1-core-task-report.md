Failed MCP jobs exposed terminal progress/failure detail but `result:null`; no native RED XML was preserved or synthesized.

The fix introduces a plan-scoped, deterministic preflight for the entire synchronous event/tick cascade before its first strategic mutation. Terminal child events and manual milestone advancement reserve a conservative remaining-plan revision budget; direct completion/failure, tracking, and reservation callbacks preflight their own exact immediate increments when no outer cascade is active. A nested callback for the same plan uses the already-preflighted outer budget; another plan must preflight independently. `StartOrWaitForMilestone` is the outer boundary for all goal submissions and reservation creation. `CompleteMilestoneAndAdvance` covers milestone release/next-stage transitions; `CompletePlan` and `FailPlan` cover their status and release events. Existing `CancelPlan` and public reservation operations retain their exact preflights. No revision saturation or wraparound was introduced, so old control tokens cannot become valid through a clamped revision.

Call-path audit: `TrackChildGoal` occurs only through `SubmitTrackedGoal` or its synchronous `GoalStarted` event while `submittingPlan` is set. `SubmitTrackedGoal` is reached only inside `CreateGoalsForMilestone` or `EnsureRecoveryGatherers`, both under `StartOrWaitForMilestone`. Reservation creation occurs only at `TryReserveRequirements` under the same boundary. Reservation release occurs under guarded milestone/plan completion or failure, or through the earlier exact-preflight cancellation, public release, and zero-amount update paths. The remaining-plan budget is `2 + active reservations + 2×existing child IDs + Σ remaining milestones (32 + 8×tactical requests + 8×resource requirements)`. The per-stage base covers canonical resource creation/release (at most four resource types), status and terminal transitions; per-request terms cover tracking and immediate terminal handling; per-requirement terms cover recovery-gatherer submission. It is intentionally conservative near `int.MaxValue` and may reject a transition that could fit a smaller exact path rather than risk partial mutation.
