## Task-4 acceptance cautions

The plan's RED list is still justified: current schema cannot express ALL current-Wood workers or the composed tree-west-of-TC selector; repeated anchored count has a graph seam but needs an exact House fixture and native distinct-site progress proof; garrison commands exist but Commander attribution and partial shelter truth do not; and discovery is not point scouting. Fixtures and native source mechanics are not themselves evidence of newly adapted Commander behavior. Keep the phase's required owner/capacity/fog/loss/exhaustion/cancel/reset checks distinct from parser/compiler-only green tests.
